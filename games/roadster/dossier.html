    Research notes

    The 2017 concept and original 2008 Roadster are not the current vehicle. Do not substitute their battery, mass, seating or drivetrain figures.

    The reported 0.81-second reveal run and 0.85-second briefing figure are not interchangeable test results. An under-1-second claim does not supply a force curve.

    Tank volume alone does not establish pressure, gas mass, thrust or flight time. A 6-second Thrust burn is not a verified Rocket flight duration. Sustained aircraft-like flight remains outside our evidence.

    The supplied Verge article could not be retrieved; no specifications are attributed to its unread text. Tesla’s current page has ambiguous upgrade footnotes; preserve trim-level scope rather than treating everything as standard.
